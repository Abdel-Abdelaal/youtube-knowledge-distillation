# The NH Checklist

Yes/no checks built from the key principles in *The Bodybuilding Book*. Use it to test a program, a claim or an idea against what Natural Hypertrophy teaches: **yes = consistent with his view**. Each check links to its principle (statement, numbers, scope, confidence, sources). A "no" is not automatically wrong: read the principle's scope first (beginner vs advanced, goal, natural).
