# 14. Life philosophy, culture & manga

This chapter covers what NH teaches beyond training: how to live, what to value, how to handle relationships, and the anime, manga, books and physical-culture history that shaped him. His core position is that lifting is one half of a complete life: body and mind must be built together, struggle beats comfort, you own your life completely, and strength only counts once it serves others. His philosophy moved from Stoicism (2020) through Nietzsche (2022-23) to Jung (2024), and much of this chapter is opinion, not training method. Claims about real people are his unverified opinions.

## Body and mind: the complete man

The base of his worldview is that the body is not secondary. Training is a spiritual practice, but it does not fix your life by itself.

### PHI-01 · Build body and mind together
- **Statement:** Body and spirit must be developed together; the ideal is the "complete man" or "educated barbarian". Training without reading and thinking is as unbalanced as theory without a body.
- **Numbers:** Training 10 h/day without reading "won't even produce proper gains".
- **Scope:** Everyone, especially men; applies to lifters and to intellectuals alike.
- **Confidence:** high — ~20 videos, 2020→2023
- **Status:** changed — 2020: the body is "the lesser part", an envelope ("you are not your body"); 2021-23: body and soul are one coin and the body belongs at the centre of life (see PHI-03).
- **Check:** Does the program or advice treat training as part of a whole life that also includes reading, thinking and other people?
- **Sources:** {{j2u5TzD5fGg@442}} {{QTxLU8WTx6g@64}} {{O-x2v688wrg@1070}} {{TPcnP8Tvj5E@307}}

### PHI-02 · Modern life inverted the mind-body balance
- **Statement:** Modern life is almost all mind and little body, while antiquity was balanced. He blames mind-body dualism (traced to Christianity's view of the body as impure) and schools built to train desk workers, and wants physical education restored.
- **Numbers:** ~95% mind / 5% body today vs ~50/50 in antiquity; 2 h of school PE a week is not enough.
- **Scope:** Society, schools, children.
- **Confidence:** medium — ~4 videos, 2022→2023
- **Status:** current
- **Sources:** {{4W_D07eE29I@563}} {{3W-yE2pgTec@696}} {{pBszDfFvoVI@190}} {{pBszDfFvoVI@317}}

## Health, longevity and aging

Health comes before muscle, and the purpose of a lifetime of lifting is to stay strong and mobile into old age.

### PHI-11 · Health comes before muscle
- **Statement:** Being muscular is pointless if you die in your 50s; sickness takes the muscle anyway. Natural bodybuilding is also about a long life. Great genetics don't excuse neglecting health (he cites Apollon, a lifelong smoker who died young of throat cancer).
- **Numbers:** 350 lb isn't healthy even at 6% body fat.
- **Scope:** All lifters, especially naturals.
- **Confidence:** high — ~8 videos, 2020→2024
- **Status:** current
- **Check:** Does the approach put long-term health ahead of maximal size?
- **Sources:** {{ylbxNE-2Im0@1040}} {{XAcv427WvMw@1230}} {{so6MbvvtM1w@1045}} {{-OjJEzZTXtQ@1324}}

### PHI-12 · Muscle is your lifeline in old age
- **Statement:** People lose the ability to walk without glute, hamstring and quad muscle. He wants to be mobile and strong at 70-80 and keep the ability to squat and hinge heavy, even with less volume and intensity. "We all age but we don't have to get old."
- **Numbers:** Lifelong trainees should live healthy to 80-85; dying at 70 is "a tragedy"; mobile and strong at 70-80.
- **Scope:** Lifelong trainees.
- **Confidence:** high — ~9 videos, 2020→2024
- **Status:** current
- **Sources:** {{TIsnQ03FegQ@100}} {{mhizcFzRp5I@439}} {{ckDmNEFcwW8@628}} {{-OjJEzZTXtQ@1386}}

### PHI-13 · Decline comes from inactivity, not age
- **Statement:** The age at which people say their body "stopped working" keeps falling; someone who takes care of himself should still thrive at 40, 50, 60 and beyond. Old men who keep their fire share a lifelong passion, physical activity and a family.
- **Numbers:** "Body stopped working" age falling from 50s → 40s → 30s → 20s.
- **Scope:** Everyone.
- **Confidence:** medium — ~6 videos, 2020→2023
