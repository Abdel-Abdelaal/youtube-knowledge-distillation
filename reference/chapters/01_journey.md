# 1. His journey & stats

This chapter records who Natural Hypertrophy is as a lifter: where he started, how his bodyweight, physique, programming and mindset changed over ~15 years, and the stats he has given for himself. The facts describe his own history, not advice; he repeatedly calls himself a "special case" whose volume most people do not need. His overall story is a weak, bullied teen who built a 6 ft, ~220 lb natural physique slowly, lost years to strength chasing, dirty bulking and injuries, and moved to self-written, hypertrophy-only training with more balance in life.

## Bodyweight history and bulking lessons

His weight story runs from slow teenage gains, through a dirty bulk to 240 lb and a crash cut, to a long recomp and a slow bulk to 220 lb. See the stats timeline at the end of the chapter.

### JRN-11 · Bodyweight path from 110 to 220 lb
- **Statement:** He went from about 110 lb at 15 to a fat peak of 235-240 lb around age 23, crash-cut to ~205-210, recomped at 210 for about three years, then slowly bulked to 220 lb, which he now maintains.
- **Numbers:** 110 (15) → 140 (17-18) → 150-160 (19) → 170 (20) → 185-190 (21) → 200-205 (22) → 235-240 peak (~23, 21-22% BF) → ~205-210 → mini-bulk 225 → ~215 → 210 recomp (2019-2022) → 215 (2023) → 220 (2024)
- **Scope:** his own bodyweight
- **Confidence:** high — ~25 videos, 2020→2024
- **Status:** contradicted — weight at 22 given as 200-205 or 220 lb; the 240-lb peak dated ~2017 in one video and 2018-2019 in others; in 2025 he recalled 110 kg in Dec 2019
