## Beginners

**Changed over time**

- **Small muscle groups: "later" → "from day one".** 2020: calves are unimportant for novices, and neck, traps and calves can wait until you have a solid physique {{OATtf3kPtdI@626}} {{OATtf3kPtdI@1757}}. 2023: forearm isolation is optional {{clzLnKrm6Ao@758}}. 2024 (later view): isolate forearms from day one and catch dormant weak points early {{okm6bZHKHI4@4630}} {{ZLheQPgEfoA@2410}}.
- **What "base" means.** 2020: novices should build a wide base covering all movement patterns, and in one video should focus only on bench, squat and deadlift before hypertrophy work {{7XqE7GhDsgc@438}} {{xFVva4-swKc@627}}. 2023 (later view): a real base is push-up and pull-up proficiency, and his programs skew toward bodybuilding because there is "no reason to waste years building a 'base'" {{ABTeMrXdgI4@670}} {{SZEjCsuclx8@2913}}.
- **Length of the novice phase.** 2020-04: novices shouldn't stay novices more than 4-6 months {{TwuT80wJql8@125}}. 2020-11 → 2021: prolong it to 1-2 years {{gWdAfpJIXbc@497}} {{9mnLRQsrs9k@916}}. 2024: done within a year for many {{yZC3Nsmmzb8@10549}}.
- **Smith machine.** 2020-2023: useless or harmful for beginners {{TrU2m-4yZT0@627}} {{_IBSNO56zuE@437}}. 2024 (later view, vertical press only): novices should lean heavily on the Smith vertical press because it handles stabilisation {{bRKQIOYo2u4@4770}}.
- **Lateral raises.** 2020: beginners don't need side raises {{OATtf3kPtdI@1503}}. 2022: they are in his novice shoulder example {{BhOuuyriBDw@1321}}.
- **Writing your own program.** 2020: novices should not write programs because theirs are almost always bad {{gWdAfpJIXbc@123}}. 2021: draft your own while running the novice program {{rlWIogS2Qho@0}}. 2024: start on an established program, then write your own and have a coach review it {{yZC3Nsmmzb8@8488}} {{6p2x4kDDjzs@5434}}. This is a refinement, not a flip.

**Contradictions**

- **Daily vs 3x/week full body.** He prefers "full body every day" with one heavy compound per day, and novices can recover from daily calisthenics {{G7rVTseabFU@720}} {{HrUPp573ki0@1890}}. Elsewhere: 3x/week is the default and full body needs programming knowledge novices lack {{G7rVTseabFU@660}} {{sfUkrygYNiU@190}}.
- **Squat/deadlift frequency.** Novices should squat and deadlift several times a week (using deloads) {{awNEfmfGkmY@189}}. His own routine deliberately lowers squat and deadlift frequency, and deadlifts once a week {{dfnn-YdXerg@755}} {{S0qBQppUtTQ@1540}}.
- **Intensity.** Lower intensity and volume at first, never struggling {{Bg7jQfbI1w8@380}} {{_4wWgin1dVY@317}}. But the top beginner mistake is training too easy, and novices should go to failure {{wB-ydKSfQ5k@2022}} {{yZC3Nsmmzb8@10229}}.
- **Variations.** "No variations for novices", with the paused squat as the exception {{MP01uQbcoLc@619}} {{gpFdh0eBMGc@686}}. Yet he likes novices doing variations {{sK78-yohV1s@1170}} {{7n8RMhsxEB4@125}}.
- **Evolving rep ranges.** Not suitable for beginners {{2QnD9k0SbwA@0}}. Teach them early {{ulwUVDK7RUk@314}} {{OATtf3kPtdI@1375}}.
- **Explore vs repeat.** Beginners should be "tossed into the sea" and explore {{QpTHtmI8nrU@564}}. A good novice program embraces monotony {{OATtf3kPtdI@1438}} {{dfnn-YdXerg@436}}.
- **Isolation dose.** "Would never prescribe that much isolation to a novice" {{pey1Ni6HPlY@280}}. Isolation in every program from the start {{QwoBxHI7NBU@375}}.

## Bulk cut

**Changed over time**

- **Recomp evangelist → slow bulker.** 2020-2021: recomping is "the future", better than bulk/cut, and "recomp beats bulking for me". He planned to recomp 4-5 more years (2021-05, 2022-03) {{IeYLu4_hkZY@190}} {{Zg0oUngwPK4@850}} {{QTHtVBP_36g@2934}} {{7Sp526pPxEU@1570}}. August 2022: ended the 3-year 210 lb maintenance and said his recomp growth was "minimal compared" to a bulk. He switched to a 2-3 year slow lean bulk to 215-220 lb {{9WA6eQODjHQ@0}} {{9WA6eQODjHQ@1003}} {{O9txIDHBBIk@1565}}. 2024: "perma-bulker is the way to go unless you start out fat" {{yZC3Nsmmzb8@6575}}. Later view: slow bulking.
- **Surplus necessity.** 2022: "you do not need a caloric surplus", having gained at maintenance {{9WA6eQODjHQ@315}}. 2022-2024: for skinny/lean lifters, a surplus is required ("you must gain weight to gain muscle"), and "a caloric surplus is your best weapon" {{RryibwmuOWE@1596}} {{h3U30oRVrMU@63}} {{yZC3Nsmmzb8@7595}}. Later view: a surplus is the main tool except for people with fat to trade.
- **Who should bulk.** 2020: only very skinny people should bulk {{VpPf9o5923k@63}}. 2024: lean/small and formerly chubby lifters must bulk, and maintaining is a mistake {{ZLheQPgEfoA@3039}} {{pt4mbNb7NJ0@10484}}. Later view: broader bulking.
- **Rate tightened.** 1-2 lb/month (2020-12) {{so6MbvvtM1w@865}} → 0.5-1 lb/month after his own too-fast 2023 bulk (20-30 lb in a year) {{6KA80BQ9W3k@1317}} {{XK9KjVP-5Wo@691}}. In 2024 1.5 lb/month was "good" {{fPU3nRIH8sM@9349}}.
- **Surplus size shrank.** 10-15% ceiling (2020) {{VAblRcVMjqw@248}} → 200-300 kcal, 500 "pointless" (2024) {{okm6bZHKHI4@8320}} {{ZLheQPgEfoA@8625}}. Note: in 2022 he called ~500 kcal near-optimal {{h7D6CB4p1oU@318}}.
- **Recomp floor.** 2020: recomp down to ~12-13% {{qmpvEERwXVI@437}}. 2022: don't recomp below ~14-15% {{9WA6eQODjHQ@690}}. Later view: 14-15%.
- **Maintenance as a summer tool.** Since 2024 he cycles winter bulk / summer maintenance-recomp {{bRKQIOYo2u4@7914}} {{okm6bZHKHI4@4379}}. That replaces both his 2020 "no cycles" stance and his 2023 continuous-bulk plan {{P7ADeDVghxg@1411}}.

**Contradictions**

- **Dirty vs slow bulk muscle gain.** He claims dirty bulking builds less muscle because junk lacks protein {{a02c10s3Pjk@256}} (2020). He also claims the muscle difference is "negligibly small, if it exists" {{-T8qWCI4_No@524}} (2021).
- **Target bodyweight.** Never set a goal bodyweight {{h7D6CB4p1oU@1708}} {{XK9KjVP-5Wo@691}}. But he praised Hamza's 180 lb target as "good nuanced advice" {{h3U30oRVrMU@379}} (2023-01).
- **Tall lifters.** Slow patient bulking (2023-12) {{hEuy__sCbZA@1326}} vs aggressive bulking justified (2024-12) {{pt4mbNb7NJ0@8764}}.
- **Years spent bulking.** "Bulked 14 of his 15 years" {{TvKzeUKgZcY@258}} vs a 3-year maintenance at 210 lb {{9WA6eQODjHQ@0}}.
- **Recomp vs bulk+cut efficiency.** Similar or slightly less long-term {{9WA6eQODjHQ@315}} vs losing dirty-bulk fat by recomp ~15x slower than a slow bulk {{-T8qWCI4_No@1209}}.
- **His ideal body fat.** 10-12% advised for an "otter mode" look (2023-03) {{HOgwZO70z_M@315}} vs 15-16% best for himself in the same video {{HOgwZO70z_M@1134}}. Likely physique-specific, not a real conflict.

## Cardio

**Changed over time**

- **No cardio → cardio advocate.** 2020-2021: he "does not do cardio in the conventional way", hadn't done cardio in 3 years, did all cardio with weights and never used ellipticals or steppers. Cardio was "not necessary", and 30 min lifting beats 30 min cardio {{ckDmNEFcwW8@0}} {{LCpa0h89TJM@380}} {{BBqiphYfqMA@504}} {{8ELQ2XZgpls@1394}}. From 2023-11: cardio is "S tier", 5-15 min after every session, and it "makes you a better bodybuilder". He now does the stair machine, treadmill miles and carries {{SxqjF2oPasg@1952}} {{6yW3GSskl8g@887}} {{-OjJEzZTXtQ@1197}}. Later view: do cardio.
- **Calories framing.** 2020: cardio kills gains indirectly through calories, and skinny lifters should avoid it {{6sFaB3rAY1A@2008}} {{WyxeMHovPJ8@125}}. 2024: good cardio boosts gains {{ryWJV6yN3ZE@189}}.
- **Leg-day cardio.** 2022: avoid intense leg cardio on leg days {{9y2s6kyFkPc@2128}}. 2024-12: hard cardio on top of leg training boosts recovery and conditioning {{-OjJEzZTXtQ@1134}}. Later view: it's fine.
- **Stepper/jump rope.** 2020: prefer stepper or jump rope over treadmill {{ckDmNEFcwW8@441}}. 2021: never steppers {{BBqiphYfqMA@504}}. 2024: StairMaster is S tier and jump rope is poor for bodybuilders {{ryWJV6yN3ZE@1769}} {{ryWJV6yN3ZE@757}}.
- **Running.** 2020: uphill running "unmatched" for cardio {{ZKgM-tX_ODU@62}}. 2024: running C tier, long distance D {{ryWJV6yN3ZE@316}} {{ryWJV6yN3ZE@379}}. Sprints and fartlek stay highly rated.

**Contradictions**

- **Running surface vs running.** He says best cardio is outdoors on soft ground (2020) {{ckDmNEFcwW8@503}}, yet he runs on a self-powered treadmill and ranks the treadmill D tier (2024) {{pt4mbNb7NJ0@569}} {{ryWJV6yN3ZE@693}}. The manual treadmill is his exception {{APmM-moU9z8@63}}.
- **Weights-only cardio.** "He does not recommend that people do cardio only through weight training" {{ckDmNEFcwW8@691}} vs, in the same era, he does all his cardio with weights and says high-rep work and giant sets "already provide enough" conditioning {{BBqiphYfqMA@504}} {{A55c7v1yMn4@875}}.

**Changed over time**

- **Rounded back on rows/hinges**: 2020 rounding as the weight descends on rows is bad form {{IqCdPJopcC4@187}}; he "never rounds his back" {{rIngKQqTJMs@375}} → 2024 rounded-back T-bar rows and deep rounded rack hinges at slow tempo are OK, earned exotic form {{-OYtq2AHGX8@385}} {{l6L6HlGM4F0@63}} {{uOAia_t5c_I@754}}. ## Volume

**Changed over time**

- **Overall volume, from maximalist to minimal-effective.** 2020: more work always gives better results, sit at the brink of overtraining, and naturals can't build a big body in an hour a day {{tZ9CSu2DMIE@61}} {{tZ9CSu2DMIE@248}} {{dfnn-YdXerg@855}}. From 2021: do as little as possible for as much as possible {{QL9Dh9CGOBQ@114}} {{8mGHG5C-EPE@1107}}. 2024: low on the volume spectrum, "closer to HIT than to 18-20 sets", and his earliest programs had far too many sets {{okm6bZHKHI4@5998}} {{bRKQIOYo2u4@389}}. 2025: volume serves progression only {{ACFKXT44BzM@751}}. The later view is the minimal-effective one.
- **Sets per exercise.** 2020: accessories almost always 4 sets, 2 not enough {{m_IGaiqrCvA@319}}. 2023: rule of three (2-4) {{aJ1e41y8Na4@379}}. 2024-2025: 3 for novice/intermediate, 2 for advanced, 2 per taxing lift {{6p2x4kDDjzs@2081}} {{ACFKXT44BzM@751}}. Later = fewer.
- **His own training.** 2020: 2-3 h sessions, 6 days/wk, responds to high volume {{cHYOYTgiRlM@187}} {{UieXEWMNnfI@380}}. 2023: prefers fewer quality sets made possible by high frequency {{6KA80BQ9W3k@1006}}. 2024: chest 6/wk, arms 9 each, shoulders 9-12, legs 6+6 {{okm6bZHKHI4@9295}} {{6p2x4kDDjzs@1680}}. In 2020 he attributed his legs to hundreds of thousands of pounds of volume squats {{RaGX9rPQr4k@499}}.
- **Nucleus overload.** 2020-2022: an advocated accessory method for nearly every body part he wanted to grow {{2edX06SQDnU@0}} {{SPicD3F686s@378}}. 2023: D tier, 1-2% of results {{SxqjF2oPasg@2959}}.
- **Back-off/backup sets.** 2020: "a must for hypertrophy… free tonnage" {{Rz4uzjM068M@942}}. 2023: B tier, better as a variation {{SxqjF2oPasg@1761}}. 2024: he skips them on dips and on non-priority squats {{c7hl9u3h_s0@10491}} {{c7hl9u3h_s0@6752}}.
