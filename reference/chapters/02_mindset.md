# 2. Mindset, natural vs enhanced & genetics

This chapter covers the mental side of training (discipline, adherence, goals, comparison, ego, dysmorphia), his position on performance-enhancing drugs (PEDs) and testosterone replacement (TRT), and what he thinks genetics do and don't decide. His overall stance: discipline and years of consistency, not motivation or talent, carry a natural lifter. Never take PEDs, because the gains leave with the drug and enhanced advice doesn't transfer to naturals. Genetics are real but mostly set speed and shape, not whether you can get big, so "bad genetics" and "the natural limit" are usually excuses. General life philosophy (stoicism, media habits, social life) is in ch. 14.

## Discipline and habits

He ranks discipline above motivation and treats it as a learned, daily habit. In 2024 he softens the rigidity but keeps the core.

### MND-01 · Discipline over motivation
- **Statement:** Motivation spikes and fades; discipline gets you to the gym. The top level is training without deliberation because it's simply on the schedule and you love it.
- **Numbers:** three levels: motivation → discipline → "just doing it"; after 10-15 years he is rarely motivated
- **Scope:** all lifters
- **Confidence:** high — ~14 videos, 2020→2024
- **Status:** contradicted — he also once called motivation "the most important half" alongside method, against his usual view that it is too unreliable to carry you
- **Check:** Is training run as a fixed habit rather than left to how motivated the lifter feels?
- **Sources:** {{RPUhg61IV-g@0}} {{kkbfHkyQ_q0@825}} {{GowuEmDClqY@4152}} {{z9MttM0Bq-I@380}}

### MND-02 · Motivational content replaces action
- **Statement:** Hype and motivational videos are a dopamine spike that substitutes for training and makes you passive (the "David Goggins effect"); a video should be the spark that sends you to train, not a replacement.

## Consistency and time horizon

### MND-19 · Consistency beats intensity and optimisation
- **Statement:** The park trainee doing daily chin-ups and push-ups outgains the perfect-program lifter who trains sporadically; years of steady work beat short bursts of madness.
- **Numbers:** consistent 1 year > obsessed 2 weeks (2020); a program for 10 years > burning out in 3 months (2020); 10 years done properly > 6 months of madness (2021); 2 years fairly hard > 6 months like a madman (2021); 2 years like a normal person > 2 weeks like a madman (2022)
- **Scope:** all lifters
- **Confidence:** high — ~20 videos, 2020→2024
- **Status:** current
- **Check:** Is the plan sustainable for years rather than maximal for a few weeks?
