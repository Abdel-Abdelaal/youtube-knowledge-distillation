# Aesthetics

**Bottom line**
- Bodybuilding is an art of illusion aimed at beauty, not a pile of mass. Train every muscle with aesthetic intent. Strength-only or compound-only training gives an "accidental" physique (big trunk and quads, small arms).
- Natural ideal: carry about 12-18% body fat with lots of muscle. He sits at 16-17% (2020-22) and 17-18% (2024) and calls stage leanness (5-8%) pointless for naturals.
- Limbs and the core make the physique. Big arms (triceps long head, forearms) are non-negotiable unless you have a huge V-taper. A developed core ties the body together, and oblique or ab training does not widen the waist; waist width is bone structure.
- Neck: aim for it to line up with the jaw from the front; much bigger than that looks like a ball. The yoke (traps and neck) makes a big body look powerful instead of fat.
- Goals are personal (Berserker/Herculean X-taper for him, otter, Apollonian V-taper), but imbalances must be on purpose. Judge muscles by how they look relaxed in everyday clothes (2024 tier list), not by stage flexing.
