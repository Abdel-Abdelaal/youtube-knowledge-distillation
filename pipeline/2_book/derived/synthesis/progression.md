# Progression

**Bottom line**
- Progressive overload is mandatory for naturals: something (load, reps, sets, frequency) must go up, and tonnage accumulated in a useful intensity window is the real target. Strength matters as the main way to raise tonnage, not as a goal in itself.
- His main tool is the evolving rep range (double progression): give each lift a fixed range (usually 6-10, 8-12, 10-15; compounds 4-8 to 8-12), add reps week to week, and add weight once the first set reaches the top. Later he also evolved sets: add a set, then add weight and drop a set.
- He wants slow, steady, never-stopping progress with stable tonnage. That means small jumps, no blocks or peaking, no deload-and-retry, and no lowering the weight to break a plateau.
- He is against static schemes (5x5, 3x5, 10x10) past the novice stage because they force sandbagging and hard plateaus. He is also against program hopping, swapping a lift because it stalled, and not logging.
- Stages are long (novice about 6-12 months; later stages 1-2+ years). Gains slow but don't stop, and he sees stalls as the point where growth happens.

## How his view changed
- **When to add weight:** Jan 2020: on 4×12, go up only if all four sets of 12 are comfortable {{xFVva4-swKc@188}}. From Aug 2020 on: waiting for every set to hit the top turns ERRs static {{CrT1WOhxLqU@64}}, and by 2024 only one set must reach the top {{ZLheQPgEfoA@566}}. Later view: first set at the ceiling.
- **Narrow low ranges:** 2020 he prescribed 4-6 often (4×4-6 pull-ups/dips, 3×4-6 squat) {{87CMjYjX2jY@125}} {{0yIieHQjEiA@1192}}. 2023-25: keep ≥4 reps between floor and ceiling, and 4-6 is "a recipe for plateaus" {{1TKqwXCge9E@638}} {{ACFKXT44BzM@498}}. Later view: wider ranges.
- **Very low reps:** 2020 elevator schemes of 2-5 reps on deadlift and OHP {{65rLZe9cz_0@314}}. 2022: compounds never below 4 {{BhOuuyriBDw@570}}; 2023: sub-5 sets ~5% of volume {{hEuy__sCbZA@1955}}.
- **Isolation ranges:** 2020: 3×6-10 "great" for isolation {{Tw8qA93Gf58@567}}. 2024: 6-10 too low, use 8-12/10-15 {{c7hl9u3h_s0@7318}}. Exceptions remain for hard curls (6-12, 6-10 in 2023-25) {{DA7QvIAyptw@1326}} {{ACFKXT44BzM@434}}.

## Contradictions
- **Evolving ranges for novices:** He says novices should not use them because they're hard to track and need beta-testing {{ZScs2L9rero@1190}} {{_L5wLHlFfI0@689}}, and that they are dangerous for lazy novices {{2QnD9k0SbwA@127}}. All of this is 2020-21 with no clear direction.
- **Order of overload levers (both Dec 2022):** "weight, then reps, then sets, then frequency" {{0pKnDucWcDc@1761}} vs "reps, then sets, then weight" {{z2uzpl_smSg@1195}} {{LNggSj0WBd8@1887}}.
