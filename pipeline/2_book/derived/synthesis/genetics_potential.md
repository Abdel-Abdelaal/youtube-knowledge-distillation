# Genetics & natural potential

**Bottom line**
- Genetics are real (frame, clavicles, insertions, limb lengths, hormones, joint/bone structure), but for almost everyone they decide *how fast* and *how it looks*, not *whether* you can get big. Bad method, low effort and impatience, not genes, explain most stagnation; he does not believe in non-responders (2025).
- There is no locatable natural limit: gains slow but keep coming for 10-15+ years, into the 30s-40s. The "limit" is a time/effort limit and a cultural one. You can't judge your genetics before ~1 year of serious training, and in his later view not before ~10.
- Later (2024-25) he admits more openly that freaks exist (~0.2% are true 10/10). He rates himself 5/10 and says a 5 must work twice as hard, can only reach what a 5 can, and should never copy a gifted lifter's method.
- Blaming genetics is a self-fulfilling "blackpill" that comes from bad advice, PED users and low "natural standards". Accept the hand you were dealt and play it well.
