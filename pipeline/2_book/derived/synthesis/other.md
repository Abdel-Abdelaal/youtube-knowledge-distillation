# Other

**Bottom line**
- Lifting won't make you a fighter. Skill decides fights, and you must choose between being maximally jacked and being a good fighter. Lifting is only a complement: prioritise the sport, then drills, then hypertrophy.
- Athletes need minimal programs that carry over to their sport, should keep some gym work in season, and should state their sports when submitting programs.
- Health habits he pushes: nasal breathing; break up sitting with walks; train posture actively (traps, abs, lower back); ~20 min of daily sun; cold showers; skip BFR for novices; stretching is low value, while mobility through full-range training is high value.
- Gym etiquette: let people work in on machines, don't talk to people mid-set or with headphones in, leave women alone mid-set, never step over a barbell.
- His channel content is public domain, and he says he won't recreate the channel if it is deleted.
