# Rest periods

**Bottom line**
- Rest only as long as you need to recover for the next set. Typically 2-3 min between straight sets or superset rounds (about 3-4 min after demanding rounds); more than 5 min is overkill, and 60-90 s or less turns lifting into cardio.
- Heavy low-rep strength work, especially squats, hinges and knee flexion, gets full rest as straight sets. Everything else should be supersetted or giant-setted.
- Supersets and giant sets: no rest between exercises, rest after the round. Pair unrelated or antagonist muscles so performance barely drops. They save ~35% of session time (30-40 min of a 2 h program), since 70-80% of gym time is resting.
- Build up to giant sets gradually. Cap them at 4-5 exercises and order them from most to least intense. Never circuit leg exercises back to back.

- **A superset is exercises back to back with no rest; then rest before repeating the round. ### Time efficiency and session length
- **Most gym time (70-80%) is rest; supersets reclaim it without cutting warm-ups or hard sets** — P/D; ~6 videos, 2020 → 2022; confidence: high; supersetting everything saves 30-40 min; supersetting accessories with main lifts saves ~35% without hurting progression (but taxes cardio); 40 min cut from a 2 h program without removing lifts; RDL + pull-ups as 3 straight sets with 4-min rests ≈ 24 min of rest vs ~12 min as a superset; the more sets, the more it saves {{Z-D9kRwK90I@63}} {{DQo0DGn_wI4@732}} {{XncJr9uMpWo@688}} {{Zv9QAIb5nGk@2269}}

## How his view changed
- **Rest length drifts shorter and looser.** 2020: 3-5 min for strength sets {{MCUpClPnPkQ@1126}}. 2021-2024: 2-3 min, by feel, "rest times don't matter" as long as you recover {{A55c7v1yMn4@437}} {{SxqjF2oPasg@3276}} {{ZLheQPgEfoA@5764}}. This is a mild refinement; 3-4 min after giant-set rounds persists through 2024 {{6p2x4kDDjzs@2690}}.

## Contradictions
- **Supersetting strength work.** "Don't superset strength work such as main bench or weighted chins" {{xQQJUXf58ZE@580}} {{du7JYCA2pa4@874}}, repeated in 2025 (low reps as straight sets with long rests) {{ACFKXT44BzM@1316}}, vs "even strength work can be supersetted, e.g. bench with rows; resting 5 min after bench is wasted" {{SPicD3F686s@629}}.
- **Leg pairings.** Pair leg press with leg curl and leg extension with calf raises {{Mq2TaHm_ySI@503}}, or lunges with skull crushers {{Zv9QAIb5nGk@2269}}, vs moving straight from one leg exercise to another being "a cardinal sin" {{jDk3LytTEjA@1726}}. Probably same-region circuits vs paired isolation, but he doesn't make that distinction.
- **Does supersetting hurt performance?** It is "not detrimental" and "costs nothing" {{Z-D9kRwK90I@0}} {{Mq2TaHm_ySI@760}} vs the loss growing in a long full-body day {{G7rVTseabFU@430}} and leg circuits destroying performance {{jDk3LytTEjA@1726}}.
