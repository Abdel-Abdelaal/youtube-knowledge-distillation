# Life philosophy

**Bottom line.** NH treats lifting as one half of a complete life: body and mind have to be built together. In his framing, training is a "Way", a spiritual practice and a ritual. He is no Stoic and no Nietzschean, but he borrows from both, and by 2024 he leans heavily on Jung. His recurring rules:
- Believe in free will and own your life completely.
- Seek struggle; distrust pleasure that comes without effort, comfort, porn, drugs and consumerism.
- Build an identity alone first, then put your strength at the service of family and community. Kindness only counts as a virtue when it comes from someone strong.
- Question morality, judge things by their consequences, and integrate your shadow instead of repressing it.

In practice his priorities are family, then work, then training. He now treats a job as a way to make a living rather than as his passion. He wants committed love and lifelong marriage, health and mobility into old age, and he calls himself agnostic but spiritual.
