# Muscle-specific training

**Bottom line**
- Every muscle you want big must be targeted directly. Compounds leave natural lifters with small arms, forearms, abs, calves, neck, side and rear delts, and the triceps long head. Big muscles (quads, glutes, hamstrings) can grow mostly from compounds.
- Weak points are almost always neglected muscles carrying a "tonnage debt", not bad genetics. Repay it over years with frequency, isolation placed early or supersetted (never left for the end), and patience. Don't strip the strong parts.
- His latest prescriptions (2023-2024) are more moderate than his 2020 high-volume "nucleus overload" spam: weighted, progressively overloaded isolation at about 6-15 reps. Abs and calves 2-4x/week, neck 1-2x/week on lower days, lateral raises 1x/week. Forearms are "the only thing to spam".
- Signature tools: pullovers (lats and ribcage), heavy weighted stretch for traps and forearms, French press or overhead extensions for the triceps long head, weighted sit-ups and knee raises for abs, split squats for the VMO, vertical pressing for delts.

### Upper back (general), mid back and lower back
- **The upper back is the most underdeveloped area in naturals and has the most growth potential** — O/P; ~12 videos, 2020 → 2023; confidence: high.
