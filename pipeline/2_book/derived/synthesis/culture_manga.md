# Culture & Manga

**Bottom line**
- Anime and manga (Baki, Berserk, Hunter x Hunter, Yu Yu Hakusho, shonen training arcs) got him into lifting and are why the channel exists. He has said this consistently from 2020 to 2024.
- Fictional physiques are valid goals and motivation if you sort out what is natty-achievable. Copying characters' training (sword swinging, Saitama's routine, weighted clothes) won't build the body; resistance training will.
- Male power fantasies are healthy blueprints when the hero earns his strength through work (classic shonen, Baki, Hajime no Ippo). They are toxic when power is handed out by chance (Solo Leveling, isekai).
- In Baki, training beats talent and Jack Hanma is the PED cautionary tale. In Berserk, Guts is a tragic hero whose pathologies shouldn't be copied; his deepest lesson is about relationships.
- Much of this file is media criticism and cultural opinion with no program content. Program-relevant bits are the Guts and Casca programs, goal physiques and the "copying training doesn't work" rule.
