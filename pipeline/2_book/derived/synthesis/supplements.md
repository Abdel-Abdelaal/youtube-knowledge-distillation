# Supplements

**Bottom line**
- Supplements are "D tier", worth maybe 1%, and the least important part of a bulk. Training, food and sleep do the work, and most products (he says ~90%) are useless or scams.
- Any supplement that truly builds muscle is a steroid. Test boosters, turkesterone/ecdysterone, tongkat ali, pump products and fat burners are scams or placebo.
- He takes few: creatine (daily, no loading, no cycling), MSM for joints (daily for years), bee pollen, and a scoop of whey at most once a day. Lately he adds raw garlic and ginger as "tonic herbs".
- He is against stimulants and pre-workouts: he takes no caffeine and no pre-workout, and says needing to be wired to train is a bad relationship with lifting.
- Never buy supplements or programs from fitness YouTubers. If you buy anything, take half the dose.
