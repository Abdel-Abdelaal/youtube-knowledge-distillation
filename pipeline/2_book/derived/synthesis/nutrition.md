# Nutrition

**Bottom line**
- For naturals, training drives growth and diet only fuels it. He rejects "diet is 70-80%" outright, but he says diet is the number-one factor for health and longevity.
- Calories in vs out sets bodyweight. Food quality, macros and food choice decide what that weight is. Eat mostly whole foods you cook yourself, lots of vegetables, minimal processed food, and salt your food.
- He thinks protein needs are heavily overstated. He has eaten roughly 50-140 g/day at ~210-220 lb over the years (about 100-120 g lately, ~0.5 g/lb), and he calls 2 g/kg or 2 g/lb a waste.
- His own diet is high carb (~60-80% of kcal), low fat and moderate protein, but he says macro balance is individual. Find it by experimenting and by looking at your ancestry, and drop foods that disagree with you (for him: dairy, most red meat, broccoli).
- Meal timing matters little ("D tier"). There is no anabolic window. He never trains fasted and eats a carb-rich meal 2-3 h before training. 2-3 meals a day is fine; many meals only help hardgainers.
- He doesn't count calories, cheat or do extreme diets (keto, carnivore, vegan, GOMAD, vertical). A diet must be one you can keep for life.
