# Personal journey

**Bottom line**
- Started at 14-15 as a ~100-120 lb, bullied, smallest-in-class kid who could not do a push-up; ~15 years later (2024) he is 6 ft, ~220 lb at ~17-18% BF, 18.4-in arms, 28-in thighs, naturally, and calls his genetics average (5/10).
- His own gains rate roughly halved every five years (110→170 lb ages 15-20, 170→200 ages 20-25, 200→215 ages 25-28). His best years were slow lean bulks on parents' food. His worst were the dirty bulk to 240 lb and the 2017-2019 powerbuilding plateau.
- His defining mistakes: no program, then copying others' plans; strength/"big three" chasing; dirty bulking; years of neglect of legs, triceps, calves, back and hamstrings; training through injuries. He recovered via a ~3-year recomp at 210 lb (2019-2022) and a switch to hypertrophy-focused, self-written programming.
- Training is mental-health infrastructure for him (6 days/week, 2-3 h for years). By 2024 he had moved to 5 days, less volume, more intensity, and more balance with family. He describes his method as experience-based, not study-based.
- The channel (started 2020, lockdown) exists to promote natural bodybuilding. He refused monetization for years, then from 2023 ran it on Ko-fi pledges (no ads or sponsors) once he became sole breadwinner. In 2025 he shifted into paid coaching (School of Strategy).
