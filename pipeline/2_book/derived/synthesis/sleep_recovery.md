# Sleep & Recovery

**Bottom line**
- Sleep is the most underrated part of recovery, as important as diet or more. Get 7-8 h (he said 7-8 h through 2022 and aims for 8-9 h in 2024), never trade it for productivity or entertainment, and go to bed before midnight with screens off ~1.5 h before.
- Recovery means eating, sleeping, napping and passive rest. Cold plunges and constant "active recovery" don't count; in his later videos (2024) he leans toward passive rest.
- Muscles don't need full recovery. ~48 h (up to 72 h) is typical, 90-95% recovered is enough, and even a trashed muscle is fresh in 4-5 days. Big lower-body work costs more: in 2024 he needed 72 h+.
- Soreness is a poor recovery and growth signal. He ranked it C tier in 2023 and last in 2024, and it should fade with consistent training.
- Life stress, a physical job and under-eating shrink recovery. Overtraining is really under-recovering.

### Recovery pot / bucket model
- **Damage fills a recovery pot whose size is your maximum recoverable volume; keep it relatively full, never empty outside a powerlifting peak** — P; ~3 videos, 2021; confidence: medium; there is a pot per muscle and for tendons, and pots feed each other (legs also fill the lower-back pot); time with the pot below the desirable level wastes gains; leaving it empty for days gives no hypertrophy benefit, though tendons may benefit {{p-kR3RfGBHw@40}} {{p-kR3RfGBHw@240}} {{p-kR3RfGBHw@330}} {{G7rVTseabFU@1695}}
- **Bucket math: drain to 40 and refill to 100 twice (120 points) beats one 100-to-0 crash** — P; 1 video, 2022; confidence: medium {{BJXFeQWGUuY@762}}
- **The big CNS bucket holds the muscle buckets; it spends more than any muscle but refills no faster, so leave water in the tank** — P; 1 video, 2022; confidence: medium; it keeps refilling while you train other muscles; capacity is large and trainable (people historically did 8-12 h/day of labour without deloads) {{NPlGK0-7h0A@564}} {{NPlGK0-7h0A@1626}} {{NPlGK0-7h0A@813}}
- **Most lifters overestimate their damage and so over-allocate rest; you can over-recover and miss stimulus windows** — P; ~3 videos, 2020 → 2022; confidence: medium; weeks at low intensity waste recovery; "gains happen when you rest" is a platitude, since gains are triggered in training and materialize in recovery {{-banYSTnkq4@1250}} {{BJXFeQWGUuY@126}} {{PuIp9cGKMHo@4085}} {{PuIp9cGKMHo@4211}}

## How his view changed
- **Active vs passive recovery.** In 2020-2021, light blood-flow work, calisthenics and a busy rest day ("not a couch day") aided recovery ({{tZ9CSu2DMIE@186}} {{0yIieHQjEiA@1319}} {{9jAjNyxeiXc@63}}). In 2024: "you do not need active recovery", constant active recovery keeps you fatigued, and couch rest matters ({{okm6bZHKHI4@10132}} {{Eta-sxecx9E@480}} {{wBct3Pl8OY4@637}}). Later view: passive rest, with light activity allowed.
- **His sleep target rose.** He said 7 h in 2021 ({{QTHtVBP_36g@2998}}) and 7-8 h in 2022 ({{u8IRHcUWfPs@944}}), then tried for 8-9 h in 2024 ({{ZLheQPgEfoA@4687}}). In 2025 he said hypertrophy training raises sleep need ({{0O0nG2jLadU@509}}).
- **Screens.** In 2020 blue light didn't bother him and only susceptible people needed to avoid it ({{7eZtMIgSnOE@560}}). In 2024 he said to ban screens ≥1.5 h before bed ({{o40xCjIzl14@134}}).
- **Forearm frequency.** In 2020 a grip drill was every 4-6 days, never daily ({{VUNsazYt-uo@124}}). In 2024 forearms recover fast enough for consecutive days with different curls ({{6p2x4kDDjzs@2930}}). Later view: more frequent.
- **Lower-body recovery lengthened.** In 2022 he trained legs every 2 days without soreness ({{dVssxcEh1UQ@999}}). In 2024 he needed 72 h+ and said lower body 3×/week is too much ({{JW73oft8Q60@1110}}).
- **Cold exposure de-emphasized.** In 2020 he discussed it at length as useful for sleep and circulation ({{mfweKhLM5L0@379}}). In 2024 he said it won't revolutionise recovery, and recovery is eating, sleeping and napping ({{Eta-sxecx9E@442}} {{Eta-sxecx9E@400}}).

## Contradictions
- **Full recovery needed?** In 2020: "training again before full recovery steals gains" ({{sfUkrygYNiU@315}}). In 2021-2022: you can train at 20% or 90-95% recovery and still grow ({{p-kR3RfGBHw@0}} {{BJXFeQWGUuY@1016}}). The two can be reconciled only as "not constantly".
- **Triceps recovery speed.** In 2020 his triceps recovered very fast ({{eFhwWirR70o@127}}). In 2023 triceps take long to recover, especially if untrained ({{d1eLAd5WvIA@3312}}). The first is personal and the second general, so this is possibly not a real conflict.
- **Script flags rejected as false alarms:** night-shift training before work vs no training right before bed (compatible); the "not sick in 10 years" lines (same claim); deadlift vs squat interference (consistent); the two 48 h lines (consistent).
